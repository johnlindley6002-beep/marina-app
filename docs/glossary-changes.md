## Skipped on purpose

- `features/marina/components/BerthAvailabilityMap.tsx` shows "Owner away" in its legend. The berth map is off limits, so it is unchanged. The one-line change would be "Berth holder away".
- The pre-arrival form and the arrival declaration still say "owner", because they mean the owner of the boat.
- "Guest or passenger" on the crew list means a person on board.
- "a State concession" and "the marina's concession" in the relet copy stay, since they mean the marina's own legal concession.
